Cookie policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Budget mensuel sets no advertising or social media cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to work, a sign-in cookie if you use an account and, only if you accept, a cookie-free visitor statistics tool.

Visitor statistics

If you click "Accept", the site loads Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts page views and records the referring site, browser and device type and the visitor's country. According to Cloudflare, it sets no cookies, uses no local storage and does not follow visitors from site to site. These statistics are only used to know which pages are used.

If you click "Decline", nothing is loaded. The site works the same way in both cases.

Change your choice

To delete your budget as well, use "Delete everything" in the settings or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Legal framework

These rules follow Article 45c of the Swiss Telecommunications Act, Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, the CNIL recommendations and EU Directive 2002/58/EC.
